# Northline brand sheet

One page to keep open while shooting, posting or briefing anyone.

## Idea

Northline is a considered edit of everyday essentials. The selection and the point of view are the product, not any one item. Every post carries the Northline name, never another brand's.

Don't lead with where products come from. "Curated in Istanbul" and "Curated from Turkey" are both ruled out as brand lines. Factual delivery and import terms on product pages are fine.

**Tagline (approved 2026-10-22):** "Considered everyday essentials." Use it wherever a tagline is needed. "Modern form. Rooted soul." was not approved, so don't use it.

## Logo

- **Primary:** the caret-over-line wordmark (`brand/northline-wordmark-*.svg`).
- **Small uses:** the caret mark alone (`brand/northline-mark-ink.svg`) for the bag bottom, stickers, insole stamps.
- **Don't add:** "Menswear", "Timeless | Minimal | Refined", or any year other than 2026.

## Voice

Short, calm, certain. "Two straps. Contoured footbed. Black or tan." / "Back in stock Friday." No hype words, no emoji clusters, no countdown stickers or discount banners.

## Product shoot

**Setup:** shoes on the marble floor, nothing in your hand. Window light from one side, flash off, room lights off. Camera fixed on a tripod or a stack of books. A white card on the shadow side. Wipe insoles and soles first. Stand beside the light so your shadow stays out of the frame.

**Six frames per product:**

1. Top-down pair, with the buckles leading and the insoles clean
2. 45° hero, the full shoe with empty space around it
3. Side profile, showing the sole layers and stitching
4. Buckle close-up
5. Leather grain and stitch close-up
6. On foot, from the knee down, on the marble only

**Edit:** one preset for everything: warm, slightly desaturated, lifted shadows.

**Insole branding:** keep third-party logos out of hero shots.

## Content pillars

1. **Product still:** one clean hero shot (feed)
2. **Detail:** buckle, footbed, stitching, to justify the price
3. **Proof:** real customer photos and delivery confirmations (stories)
4. **Behind the scenes:** packing an order, a parcel going out, a delivery arriving

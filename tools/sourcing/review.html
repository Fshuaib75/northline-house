What these recommendations mean

Prices and available sizes are supplier observations, not reservations. Freight weight is estimated. Verify size, material, photos and the supplier price before publishing. Rankings measure review priority or learned approval preference; they do not forecast sales.

Only explicitly approved products are included in the findings download. Nothing here publishes to your shop. Save feedback after reviewing to retain your decisions and train the model later.

Approved items can be sent to your book’s To review inbox. Sending does not publish products.

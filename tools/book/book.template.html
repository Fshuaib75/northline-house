How prices are worked out

“You pay” is the price on the source site when added (Capone and DeFacto: their own sites; other brands: n11 listing price, 2 Oct 2026). If you actually buy it for a different price (e.g. on Trendyol), type that under “Paid a different price?” on the item and press Publish: agents get the new price and what you pay stays encrypted. “Same as agent site” (on by default) shows exactly what agents see; switch it off to also see styles that have no price yet. Landed cost = lira price × ₦ rate + freight by weight (₦ per kg × the item’s shipping weight, shown next to “Landed cost”) + a handling fee per item. Default weights: slides 0.35 kg, flip-flops 0.3, sandals 0.45, clogs 0.5, loafers 0.65, sneakers 0.8, heels/platforms +0.15; bags 0.35–0.9; baby 0.12–0.25. Baby pieces earn agents ₦1,500. Selling price = landed cost + agent’s commission (₦2,000 if the item sells for ₦30,000 or less, otherwise ₦4,000) + your profit (the larger of the minimum profit and the % of landed cost), rounded up to ₦500, then dropped to a round ₦5,000 figure when that’s within ₦1,500 and you still keep at least ₦2,000. The earlier plan of paying agents 25% of the margin from 16 Oct is dropped.
